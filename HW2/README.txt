## Interactive Features Summary
1. **Project Setup & Menu:** Created and configured a First Person Unreal Engine project featuring a main menu scene with functional Start and Quit buttons.

2. **3 Levels & Layouts:** Implemented three unique levels including a menu, a target-shooting level, and a final challenge level with static meshes and materials.

3. **Targets & Health System:** Developed destructible targets with a starting health variable of 100 that automatically trigger level progression when all targets are destroyed.

4. **Hazards & Player Health:** Built hazard objects that interact with the player, tracking a 100-point player health pool and automatically reloading the level upon reaching zero.

5. **Ammo & Pickups:** Added a limited ammo counting system (`AMMO: ##`) along with custom health and ammo pickup items that restore player stats and destroy themselves on overlap.
